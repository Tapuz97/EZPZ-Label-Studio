EZPZ Label Studio

Label design and Bluetooth printing for Windows and supported web browsers. Created by Gal Mitrani.

Support

Email gm@galmitrani.com with your app version, operating system, printer model and a description of the issue. Share diagnostics only if you are comfortable sharing the device identifiers they contain.

Printer compatibility

Designed for BLE printers in the EZlabel-compatible family. BPXX DB403 printing has been physically verified in Windows and Chrome on Windows 11; other models and browser combinations need further testing. Niimbot, iPrint, Labelife, Nelko, Jadens, Pixmate, and other families remain future research candidates. A printer appearing in Bluetooth does not guarantee that its print format is supported.
